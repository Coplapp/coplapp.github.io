Features

Everything Copl does to make everyday life flow - without losing closeness.

Encrypted chat

A private chat just for the two of you - end-to-end encrypted so only you can read it. Send text, photos and short video clips. For the sensitive stuff there's "view once": the photo or clip disappears once your partner has opened it, with the option to ask to see it again. Lock the chat with PIN or Face ID, choose auto-deletion, and edit or delete messages afterwards - deleted for both of you.

Shared calendar

All events in one place - pickups, doctor visits, free evenings, work trips. Set up recurring routines like weekly cleaning or football practice and they land in the calendar automatically. Notifications appear for both of you at the same time so nothing is forgotten. Mark events private if you want something just for yourself. Export the calendar to your phone and on to Outlook, Google or iCloud - private events are always kept out. Week numbers are shown in every view.

Our to-do

Shared lists you both see in real time. Assign tasks to yourself, your partner or "whoever gets to it first". Check off with a tap - your partner sees instantly that it's done. Great for everything from pickups and bookings to "call the insurance company about the car".

Shopping lists

Create parallel lists - weekly groceries, a new thing for the home, Christmas gifts, the summer holiday. Add items from any device, check them off in the store with a swipe. Archive finished lists so you're not hunting through old notes. You both see the same lists, always.

Family

Profile cards for each family member with shoe size, clothing size, allergies, doctor contacts, name day and other things you always forget. No more digging through email or asking "what shoe size is Elsa now?" before every shopping run. All sensitive info is end-to-end encrypted.

Homework

Keep track of what needs doing, when it's due, and who's taking it. A clear deadline flag shows instantly if something has slipped so you can step in. Great for both helpful parent oversight and a fair split between the two of you.

Meal planning

Decide the week's dinners in advance so you skip the "what should we eat?" question at 5pm. Assign who cooks which day. Save favourite recipes and generate the shopping list automatically - all the ingredients for the week's meals land straight in the store list.

Weekly routines

Recurring chores like cleaning, taking out the bins, training, homework help. Check off per day and see streaks that motivate you to keep going. Great both for splitting responsibility fairly and for making visible how much each of you actually pulls.

Workouts

Log your sessions - gym, running, swimming, yoga. Set individual weekly goals and see streaks that cheer you on. A monthly reflection helps you see what works and adjust when life changes. Great for couples who train together or just want to root for each other.

Our budget

Build your household budget together - income, fixed expenses, savings goals. See monthly snapshots so you get a trend over time: are rates going up or down, are you saving more than last year, how much do you eat out? Skip the awkward "where did the money go?" talks.

Agreements

Write down what you've actually agreed on - the split of household chores, screen-time rules for the kids, what applies on holidays, how you handle money together. Clear and visible to both so it isn't forgotten or distorted over time.

Appreciations

Send short notes of gratitude to each other - "thanks for taking care of Elsa when I was sick", "love that you always have dinner ready on Tuesdays". Small texts that build up and strengthen your relationship over time. Save them as lovely memories to return to.

Reminders

When you think of something important for your partner but don't want to interrupt right now - send a scheduled reminder. "Remember the dentist on Thursday", "ask your boss about holiday", "buy flowers for mum before Sunday". Arrives when it's needed.

Closeness (opt-in)

Tools to keep closeness alive - send spontaneous wishes to each other (a hug, a kiss, a date night, a movie night) and take them back if you change your mind, preferences to explore together, an intimacy log to follow rhythm and frequency, a weekly match that offers small things to try for each other. PIN-locked and end-to-end encrypted - just between the two of you. Completely optional module.

Relationship pulse

Daily and weekly mood check-ins - how are we right now? Honeymoon, stable, a bit sluggish, stormy? See a trend graph over time so you can spot patterns and talk about it before it grows. Anonymous data if you prefer - the focus is on making it visible for yourselves.
