🔐 End-to-end encrypted where it matters

Your most sensitive things — closeness, family info, agreements, appreciations, check-ins and the entire chat (messages, photos and video clips) — are end-to-end encrypted. Only the two of you can read them. Not even we who run Copl can see the content.

Everything else (calendar, to-dos, budget, meals) is encrypted in transit and on our servers. Inside the app you can also verify your security code with each other — so you know no one is listening.
