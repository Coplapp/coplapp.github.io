Everything you share - in one place

Sixteen modules - all the practical stuff, plus what keeps you close.

Chat

A shared chat for text, photos and short clips - private and lockable.

Read moreShow less

A shared chat for text, photos and short video clips. Send photos and clips as "view once" - they disappear once the recipient has opened them, with the option to ask to see them again. Lock the chat with PIN or Face ID, choose auto-deletion of history, and edit or delete sent messages.

Shared calendar

Every event in one place, synced for you both - with export to your phone's calendar.

Read moreShow less

All events in one place - pickups, doctor visits, free evenings, work trips. Set up recurring routines and they land in the calendar automatically. Notifications appear for both of you at the same time. Mark events private if you want something just for yourself. Export the calendar to your phone and on to Outlook, Google or iCloud - private events are always kept out. Week numbers are shown in every view.

Our to-do

Shared lists in real time - assign, check off and break down bigger tasks.

Read moreShow less

Shared lists you both see in real time. Assign tasks to yourself, your partner or "whoever gets to it first". Check off with a tap - your partner sees instantly that it's done. Great for everything from pickups and bookings to "call the insurance company about the car". Break bigger tasks into subtasks - turn e.g. "Plan the 40th birthday" into an activity with a checklist.

Shopping lists

Several lists at once that you both see and check off in the store.

Read moreShow less

Create parallel lists - weekly groceries, a new thing for the home, Christmas gifts, the summer holiday. Add items from any device, check them off in the store. Archive finished lists. You both see the same lists, always.

Family

Profile cards with sizes, allergies and key contacts - encrypted.

Read moreShow less

Profile cards for each family member with shoe size, clothing size, allergies, doctor contacts and name day. No more digging through email before every shopping run. Family notes are end-to-end encrypted.

Homework

Track what's due, when and who's taking it - with clear deadlines.

Read moreShow less

Keep track of what needs doing, when it's due and who's taking it. A clear deadline flag shows instantly if something has slipped so you can step in.

Meal planning

Plan the week's dinners, save recipes and auto-build a shopping list.

Read moreShow less

Plan the week's dinners in advance and assign who cooks which day. Save good dishes to "Our recipe bank" and add them to a future week's plan with one tap - pick the day. Mark a dish as needing shopping and a shopping list is created automatically. Week numbers and "copy last week" are included too.

Weekly routines

Recurring chores you check off and split fairly.

Read moreShow less

Recurring chores like cleaning, taking out the bins, training and homework help. Check off per day and see streaks. Great for splitting responsibility fairly and showing how much each of you pulls.

Workouts

Build your program, run sessions in the app and track your progression.

Read moreShow less

Build your own program - choose how many sessions a week and fill each one with exercises from the exercise bank, or let Copl generate a ready-made split for you. Run the session in the app and log sets, reps and weight; you see what you lifted last time so progression is clear. Animated exercise guides show how each movement is performed. Set individual weekly goals, follow streaks and see what share of weeks you hit your goal - plus a monthly reflection.

Our budget

Build the household budget together with clear status and trend.

Read moreShow less

Build your household budget together - income, fixed expenses, savings goals. A colour-coded status shows instantly whether the budget is balanced, has money left to allocate or is over budget. Monthly snapshots give a trend over time.

Agreements

Write down what you've agreed on - visible to you both.

Read moreShow less

Write down what you've agreed on - the split of household chores, screen-time rules, what applies on holidays, how you handle money. Clear and visible to both so it isn't forgotten or distorted over time.

Notes

A shared place for thoughts, lists and ideas, always in sync.

Read moreShow less

A shared place for your thoughts, lists and ideas - what to buy for the party, name suggestions, things to fix around the house, anything that doesn't fit in a calendar or to-do. Write together, always in sync between you.

Appreciations

Short notes of gratitude that strengthen you over time.

Read moreShow less

Send short notes of gratitude to each other. Small texts that build up and strengthen your relationship over time. Save them as memories to return to.

Reminders

Send a scheduled reminder to your partner about something important.

Read moreShow less

Send a scheduled reminder to your partner about something important - "remember the dentist on Thursday", "buy flowers for mum". Arrives when it's needed, without interrupting right now.

Closeness (opt-in)

Optional, PIN-locked tools to keep closeness alive.

Read moreShow less

Tools to keep closeness alive - send spontaneous wishes (a hug, a kiss, a date night, a movie night) and take them back if you change your mind, preferences to explore together, an intimacy log to follow rhythm and frequency, and a weekly match. PIN-locked and completely optional.

Relationship pulse

Mood check-ins that show your trend over time.

Read moreShow less

Daily and weekly mood check-ins - how are we right now? Honeymoon, stable, a bit sluggish, stormy? See a trend graph over time so you can spot patterns and talk about it before it grows.
